## Priority issues

1. [P1] The suggestion fails at the point of use: 145.51 lb × 5 for an lb lifter (a 2.5 kg step converted); the plank tagged Add time at the 60 s it was judged from; a Coach plan Why repeats the note and shows no basis, so it cannot reveal a claim History contradicts. Fix: round steps to the unit's plate jump; hold when nothing changes; end every Why with its basis in the log's notation. Command: /impeccable harden.
2. [P2] Moving on is the logger's weakest action: after the plan's sets, Complete is an underlined word while the dock offers Set 5; after Complete the dock's button is Reopen; the next exercise is only the top-left back link. Fix: Complete becomes the dock's primary once the plan's sets are in, then Back to the day (optionally Next: X as a choice). Command: /impeccable layout.
3. [P2] The check-in's scales flip direction: Quality 1 poor → 5 great, fatigue and soreness 1 good → 5 bad. Fix: draw Quality great → poor without changing what is stored, or echo the chosen word. Command: /impeccable clarify.
4. [P2] Discard empty session is one tap, irreversible, and not always empty (a check-in goes with it). Fix: Undo on the note, or confirm in place; name the check-in when there is one. Command: /impeccable harden.
5. [P2] Today opens on a deficit: "27 days behind" first, with nothing to act on; the squares stay 9 pt at 200%. Fix: say behind where it can be acted on (Programme: start the plan from today); grow the squares with the text. Command: /impeccable clarify.
