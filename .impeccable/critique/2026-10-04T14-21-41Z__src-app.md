## Priority issues

1. [P1] Today is one undifferentiated list, not a day of activities: six three-line rows under "Lower A", then Run, Ride and Swim below the fold at 402×874; the workout's Resume pinned far from its status line. Fix: one card per activity in one style (mark, name, time, target, gym), collapsible, each with its own action. Command: /impeccable layout, /impeccable distill.
2. [P1] The coach's words are cut and repeated: CoachNote clamp without `more` (today-view.tsx:323, workout-overview.tsx:450); 5 of 6 exercise notes cut mid-word at 320; logger's More opens Why, which repeats the note behind it and adds a History row duplicating the tab. Fix: expand in place; say each sentence once; drop Why's History row. Command: /impeccable distill, /impeccable clarify.
3. [P1] Today's actions ignore an open session: Start workout lands on the other open session; More offers ad hoc, re-plan and skip mid-session; the gym chip stays live; finishing 0 sets is not refused (repositories/sessions.ts finishSession). Fix: derive actions from one state; warn at 0 sets. Command: /impeccable harden.
4. [P2] Discard empty session: centred full-width bold text button between the status line and the list, instant and silent; at 320×568 it touches the pinned Resume. Fix: under the workout card's action, left-aligned, with feedback. Command: /impeccable clarify.
5. [P2] The workout does not say what is next, and the warm-up is not a control: no primary action at 0 sets, no chevrons, warm-up done only through its sheet. Fix: a Mark done control on the warm-up row, chevrons. Command: /impeccable clarify.
