## Design specificity verdict

Two products in one. The record half (prints, the month, the Food bowl, one graph component,
strict black and white, Jost and Atkinson) is authored for Overload. The planning and coach half
falls back on form-app habits: stacked grey boxes, boxes in boxes, the browser's triangle, and
paragraphs of explanation; the AI coach hub (uppercase eyebrow, attention card) is the least
specific screen. A run's print is the same runner every day.

## Priority issues

- **[P1] The decision screens are essays.** The coach's draft review puts about 700 words and
  every session in full before Start (12,723 px). Decision first; reasoning in the coach note,
  doubts in a fold with their count, weeks as one line per session.
- **[P1] "How it works" text on every screen off the core loop.** Privacy, heart-rate zones, Edit
  profile, intakes, builders, session pages, AI coach and setup explain themselves; 65 field hints
  print help, 23 raw `<details>` bypass the fold component. Cut, or move into an ⓘ or a fold.
- **[P1] Lists cannot tell sessions apart, and runs are owed twice.** Plan, Log and History name
  rows "Run" / "Unplanned session"; a strength day's run and the running programme's run both
  stand. Name by kind with the figure; fix the double booking at the source.
- **[P2] Planning screens fall out of the design system.** Boxes everywhere, three folds, three
  tick styles, three date formats, terminology drift, a 10 px uppercase eyebrow.
- **[P2] Today opens on a debt count that does not reconcile** with Plan's "Week 1 of 8" and the
  programme's "56 days left".
