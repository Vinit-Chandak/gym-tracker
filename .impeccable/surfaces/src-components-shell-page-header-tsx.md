# Headings and blocks: event tickets

Scope: every screen's title, section headings, card titles and the blocks they head (Plan, a programme's page, Profile, Today first), as mock-ups on the Design canvas. Visitor mode: Operate. Owner's choice on 28 October 2026: the art of Games (Munich 1972 pictograms), the overall design of the Coupon book, toned down with the category standard's restraint.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every session is a ticket to its event: a white ticket whose stub carries the session's date and its sport's pictogram on the sport's colour, filled when done; a programme is a season pass. It refuses the category default of grey grouped lists with coloured icon chips under a gradient hero card.

OWN-WORLD: Munich 1972 event tickets and signage. Pale blue-grey ground #dfe5ec, white tickets with notched stubs and no outlines or shadows, sport colour fields (ultramarine strength, burnt orange run #bd560a, violet ride, viridian swim, rose mobility, cadmium food), Aicher-grid pictograms (0, 45 and 90 degrees, one stroke, disc heads) in white on full colour or in colour on tints. Host Grotesk for titles, headings and figures (tabular by default); Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for words; one ink action.

STORY: The athlete reads what is owed as tickets in date order, knows each sport by pictogram and colour at a glance, sees what is done filled in, and opens a ticket to act. The season pass says the race, the week and the sessions left.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Plan: "This week" in Host Grotesk 36/720 at the gutter, dates and "1 of 4 runs done" under it; seven day tabs with sport bands, today in ink; Last week and Next week pills; one ticket per session (stub: date and pictogram; body: name and figure); "Programmes" with + New; the season pass in burnt orange with the runner pictogram as art, race date, ten week squares and its stub "38 left"; the tab bar.

FORM: Grounded candidate 1 (Munich 1972 signage) fused with the dealt challenger vernacular-ephemera-jet-age-ticket-wallet and the category standard's restraint, by the owner's choice. seed:9319cd879c9732f6

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
