Where the figures come from

Every name and figure is the repository’s, and so is every interface string the app already has. Where a figure is worked out (a warm-up ramp, a pace, a total), it is worked out with the app’s own rule. Today follows the preview’s Friday; the session pages follow Upper A’s third cycle, by cycle because its test has no dates.

  • Today and its run: the preview Today (src/app/(preview)/preview/page.tsx), Fri 11 Sept 2026, Easy Run + Arms.
  • The programme: src/db/seed/data/program.ts (8-Week Strength + Aesthetics Hybrid) and its warm-ups (warmups.ts).
  • The session, from Workout to Summary: Upper A in src/server/repositories/sessions.test.ts. Its third cycle (60 × 4 @ 2) and the Hold it earns; the warm-up ramp from src/domain/warmup-ramp.ts.
  • Progress, the calendar, History, Running, Body and Recovery: the audit database for vinit on 29 Sept 2026, as scripts/dev/audit.mjs seeds it.
  • Gyms, machines and the profile: the same audit account (93 equipment types at Anytime Fitness, height from the coach intake, no date of birth or goal yet).
  • Food: the food preview (Fri 25 Sept 2026) and its over state.
  • The coach: scripts/dev/seed-audit.ts (vinit’s question, notes and memo) and the coaching preview’s proposed change.
  • Friends: scripts/dev/seed-people.ts. The first run: the onboarding pages and equipment-types.ts.
  • Search results: src/lib/exercise-search.ts, run over the 276 seeded exercises.

What the reviews found on the last canvas, and what changed

  • A set showed Saved before it was saved: the mark now turns while the server answers, Save reads Saving…, and the ink lands on the answer.
  • Logging lost the entry at 320 pt and at 200% text: the session covers the tab bar, the steppers keep one row down to 360, stack below it, and the 200% board keeps the equipment, the target and the suggestion.
  • No typed entry: tapping a figure types it, on the decimal pad.
  • “@” meant both RIR and load: it is RIR only; a load the coach set leads the line.
  • Two breaks of the Black-and-White rule: the weight chart’s point and text selection are ink.
  • Prints restated their lists and their shapes did not line up: one module grid and one ground; no words on prints; the rows are their legend.
  • Actions hid in the overflow: Technique and History are tabs, Add set ends the ledger, Add exercise and Superset sit under the workout’s list, and Today’s More options is drawn.
  • No Android size and no safe-area rule: Android boards, inset rules and a platform board.
  • The end of a session was not drawn: Finish, then the summary with its print and records.
  • Row and Racket broke One Cut: every variant is now a single cut.

What the fresh review and the audit found on this canvas, and what changed

  • The wave sank through the ground and a skipped swim looked done: waves stand on the ground, a skipped crest is dashed with square ends, and the wheel and fan are whole modules.
  • Prints lined up three ways: every print starts at the left with its parts in the order of its rows.
  • Pounds were read aloud as kilograms: every set names its own unit.
  • The session pages followed three sessions with misattributed sets: Workout, Log, Why, Finish and Summary follow Upper A, cycle 3 (60 × 4 @ 2), from the app’s own test.
  • The calendar was a hidden picture: every day is a link that names what it holds, with its marks under its own date.
  • The 200% log dropped controls: it keeps More, Set options, Hold, every set and Add set, with reps and RIR on the first screen.
  • “@” meant RIR and RPE, and records were colour tiles: RPE is written “RPE 8”, and records are an ink list.
  • The kettlebell read as the padlock: free weights are a dumbbell.
  • Copy and data that were not the app’s: the boards use the app’s own strings, and gyms, machines, search results and the profile come from the same audit account.
  • Targets under 44 pt, weak contrast and tabs that were links: hit areas grow to 44 (48 dp on Android), future days, bars and step dots meet 3:1, and only real tabs are tabs.
  • Text said twice and labels clipped: status words, a second “Coach”, a second back link and Body’s second reading are gone; Progress picks its section from one button and shows its range.
  • Kept on purpose: the RIR stepper and the 64-pt tab bar, as your notes asked. Sets still save only when the server answers, as the app does, and the check-in scales keep the app’s direction, with their end words shown.
