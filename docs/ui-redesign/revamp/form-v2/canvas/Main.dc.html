Where the figures come from

Every name, figure and line of copy is the repository’s. Where a figure is worked out (a warm-up ramp, a pace, a total), it is worked out with the app’s own rule.

  • Today and its run: the preview Today (src/app/(preview)/preview/page.tsx), Fri 11 Sept 2026, Easy Run + Arms.
  • The programme: src/db/seed/data/program.ts (8-Week Strength + Aesthetics Hybrid) and its warm-ups (warmups.ts).
  • Logging the bench: src/server/repositories/sessions.test.ts (Upper A over three cycles and the Hold it earns); the warm-up ramp from src/domain/warmup-ramp.ts.
  • Progress, the calendar, History, Running, Body and Recovery: the audit database for vinit on 29 Sept 2026, as scripts/dev/audit.mjs seeds it.
  • Food: the food preview (Fri 25 Sept 2026) and its over state.
  • The coach: scripts/dev/seed-audit.ts (vinit’s question, notes and memo) and the coaching preview’s proposed change.
  • Friends: scripts/dev/seed-people.ts. Gyms and machines: src/db/test/fixtures.ts. The first run: the onboarding pages and equipment-types.ts.
  • Search results: src/lib/exercise-search.ts, run over the 276 seeded exercises.

What the reviews found on the last canvas, and what changed

  • A set showed Saved before it was saved: the mark now turns while the server answers, Save reads Saving…, and the ink lands on the answer.
  • Logging lost the entry at 320 pt and at 200% text: the session covers the tab bar, the steppers keep one row down to 360, stack below it, and the 200% board keeps the equipment, the target and the suggestion.
  • No typed entry: tapping a figure types it, on the decimal pad.
  • “@” meant both RIR and load: it is RIR only; a load the coach set leads the line.
  • Two breaks of the Black-and-White rule: the weight chart’s point and text selection are ink.
  • Prints restated their lists and their shapes did not line up: one module grid and one ground; no words on prints; the rows are their legend.
  • Actions hid in the overflow: Technique and History are tabs, Add set ends the ledger, Add exercise and Superset sit under the workout’s list, and Today’s More options is drawn.
  • No Android size and no safe-area rule: Android boards, inset rules and a platform board.
  • The end of a session was not drawn: Finish, then the summary with its print and records.
  • Row and Racket broke One Cut: every variant is now a single cut.
