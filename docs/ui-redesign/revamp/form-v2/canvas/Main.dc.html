Form v2, refined

The art is your training. The interface is black and white and stays out of the way; every colour is a print made from what was logged. This canvas redraws Form after your notes: a new alphabet with the run as a track, logging as the sets themselves, the old calendar back on its paper, and the rest of the app in the same language.

Where the figures come from

Every name and figure is the repository’s, and so is every interface string the app already has. Where a figure is worked out (a warm-up ramp, a pace, a total), it is worked out with the app’s own rule. Today follows the preview’s Friday; the session pages follow Upper A’s third cycle, by cycle because its test has no dates.

  • Today and its run: the preview Today (src/app/(preview)/preview/page.tsx), Fri 11 Sept 2026, Easy Run + Arms.
  • The programme: src/db/seed/data/program.ts (8-Week Strength + Aesthetics Hybrid) and its warm-ups (warmups.ts).
  • The session, from Workout to Summary: Upper A in src/server/repositories/sessions.test.ts. Its third cycle (60 × 4 @ 2) and the Hold it earns; the warm-up ramp from src/domain/warmup-ramp.ts.
  • Progress, the calendar, History, Running, Body and Recovery: the audit database for vinit on 29 Sept 2026, as scripts/dev/audit.mjs seeds it.
  • Gyms, machines and the profile: the same audit account (93 equipment types at Anytime Fitness, height from the coach intake, no date of birth or goal yet).
  • Food: the food preview (Fri 25 Sept 2026) and its over state.
  • The coach: scripts/dev/seed-audit.ts (vinit’s question, notes and memo) and the coaching preview’s proposed change.
  • Friends: scripts/dev/seed-people.ts. The first run: the onboarding pages and equipment-types.ts.
  • Search results: src/lib/exercise-search.ts, run over the 276 seeded exercises.

Critiques

Three fresh-eyes reviews, each blind to the last: G read the canvas before round 1’s fixes, J the canvas after your second notes, and K the canvas after J’s fixes. Each scores Nielsen’s ten heuristics from 0 to 4 (40 in all), checks cognitive load and your notes one by one, and measures every board in a browser; a string or figure traced to the app’s code is not counted against the design. Why the scores were low

  • Consistency (2 of 4 in G and J): the same thing said two ways. Prints aligned three ways; “@” meant RIR and RPE; the log’s columns stopped matching the entry at 360 and 320; a bar under the art meant indoors; six lists started their names away from the edge.
  • Recognition (2 of 4): codes you had to remember. The indoors bar had no key; tapping the dash for the target, or a figure to type, was not shown or named.
  • Match with the real world (2 of 4 in G): “63 × 9 @ 2 × 2”, “≤ 297 g”, and prescriptions without the app’s own “RIR”.
  • Help (2 of 4 in G): nothing taught the alphabet, and RIR was never explained on screen.
  • At 200% text, RIR fell below the first screen.

Kept on purpose

  • The seven cycle squares stay wordless, as your note asked; their name is read aloud.
  • A superset shows no rest before its first set, as every session does.
  • After a save the entry goes back to the suggestion, as the app does.
  • Edit profile’s Save stays live; the app checks on submit.

What the second review found, and what changed

  • Logging at 200%: the whole entry stays docked above the home indicator, with the log scrolling above it; the dash is a button; RIR over its target.
  • The set number has its own 18-pt column in the log and the entry, so “102.5” never touches it; the log’s figures step down the type ramp to fit.
  • No line under any art: the indoors bar is gone from every form and calendar mark; a row’s glyph says treadmill or outdoors.
  • The log’s place: J wanted no gap between the log and the entry, and your question about the room above the sets settled it: the log fills down from the tabs towards the entry, which stays docked at the foot with Save.
  • Only warm-ups done are drawn; the entry says “Warm-up 2 of 3”. Choosing RIR no longer makes the suggested load look confirmed.
  • At 360 and 320, + stands over each figure and − under it, so the log’s columns hold; every log line is 44 pt or more.
  • Every figure is a named button (“62.5 kilograms, suggested. Type a load”); ⓘ beside RIR gives the app’s own explanation; a tap on the waiting Save shows the app’s sentence for a missing RIR.
  • The app’s notation: “3 × 8–12 @ 1–2 RIR”, “63 kg × 9, 63 kg × 9”, “86 / 297 g”.
  • Names at the edge on Dinner, search results, a gym’s machines, Privacy and the coach’s change, which is ruled sections now, not cards; “was” and “becomes” are read aloud.
  • 3:1 for the empty-day dot and a warm-up block’s edge; meal marks edged in ink; today’s ring clear of its date.
  • The Moment: the dial refills at 3:00 and its name follows; words fade out in 80 ms and in in 120.
  • One way each: the coach note while planning, the gym as a pin, chart scales on the left, Portion’s print square, round steppers at 200%, Filters a funnel, the indoor bike redrawn, “instead of” and “Planned by the coach” in words.
  • Continuity: the rest timer is on in Profile; Progress has no stray strip; only 25 Sept opens the Day; Today → Check-in → Workout → Log and Finish → Summary are linked.

What the third review found, and what changed after it

  • A save that fails is drawn where it happens (Log-Failed): the entries stay in ink, nothing is added to the log, rest does not start, and the app’s sentence stands over Save.
  • The RIR ⓘ’s target no longer touches the figure above it or the buttons below.
  • At 200% the reps show their target (3–5) under them, as RIR shows its own.
  • Typing keeps column-wide 44-pt figures and the entry’s rule, with the log 8 pt above it.
  • The Moment starts from the log at rest, as Log shows it.
  • Logging no longer says anything twice: the header gives the range a set aims at (3–5 reps); how many sets and the RIR target are the entry’s.
  • Training draws the whole week on one module, so the days compare at a glance.
  • The coach’s change opens on its reason, the coach’s own words.
  • Platforms and DESIGN.md match the boards: the 200% layout, the ramp’s figure sizes, Figure XL, a track’s length in modules.
  • Smaller: Send note waits for a note; steps not started meet 3:1; body weight and height take the decimal pad; meal figures say kcal; the row under way says how far it is; a past workout lists its sets as the app does.

What the review of the changed boards found, and what changed after it

  • The entry docks at the foot again, whole, and the log fills down from the tabs towards it: no room above the sets, and nothing a thumb needs moves when a set lands.
  • Every message about Save stands in one slot over it (the missing RIR, a failed save, a superset’s “Then Wrist curl”), so Save never moves; a missing RIR also inks the dash.
  • The RIR ⓘ is a full 44-pt target again (48 dp on Android), 4 pt clear of the figure and the buttons, with the three columns’ buttons on one line.
  • A failed save reads Retry, as the app’s does.
  • At 200% the first screen names the exercise and its range; the board has its main landmark.
  • The Moment turns to the next set on the beat the line lands, so the saved set is not shown twice.
  • Technique and History give the whole prescription; Training’s next day is said once; AI coach’s More sits on the gutter; Platforms describes the steppers as the boards draw them.
