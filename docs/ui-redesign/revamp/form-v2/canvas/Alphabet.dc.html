One cut per sport

A sport the app adds later is its family’s form with one cut, never an addition: the paper shows through where the cut is. A variant never touches the ground; that belongs to context.

State

Three states, the same on every form: thinned is to do, full is done, a dashed edge is skipped. To do keeps an edge of its full pigment, so to do and done differ in more than lightness.

  • To do: thinned, with an edge of the full pigment

  • In progress: done from the bottom up

  • Done: full pigment

  • A warm-up: done, but not counted; grey

  • Skipped: a dashed edge

One grid, one ground

Every print is laid out on one module: a set of lifting is a square module; an exercise is a column of its sets standing on the ground; a superset’s two columns stand closer than any others. Every other form stands on the same ground, as tall as a whole number of modules, so tops and rows line up. Shapes never overlap, and a print carries no words.

A day

The day’s print is Today’s hero and every record’s header: what is owed, thinned, inking as it is done.

Today, Fri 11 Sept: to do. Four arm exercises (the forearms pair closer) and the run.

Upper A in progress: the bench’s first two sets inked, the warm-up done.

Fri 25 Sept, done: a lift with its warm-ups in grey, a run and an open-water swim.

A month

  • Every activity of a day is in its cell, two to a row, bottom up; past four, +N.
  • Today is ringed in ink; days after today stay blank; a day with nothing has a dot.
  • The eighth column is each week’s sessions; this week’s says so far.
  • Tap the month for the whole calendar: the same cells, larger, one month after another.

The bowl

The bowl is the day’s target. Each meal is a layer, by area. Past the target the food heaps over the rim as one symmetric mound, so over reads at a glance without a second colour or a broken shape.

Fri 25 Sept, under: three meals, 1,152.5 of 2,300 kcal.

The same day with dinner (the preview’s over state): 2,536 kcal, the heap above the rim.

The limit: at twice the target the heap closes the circle.

Rules

  • Drawn from recordsA print is made only from what the account logged; nothing decorative, nothing guessed.
  • No words on a printThe rows under it are its legend, each led by a small copy of its part.
  • One groundEvery form stands on one ground line; tops and rows line up on the module grid.
  • Colour is sportThe interface is black and white; pigment means a family, nowhere else.
  • One cutA new sport is its family’s form with one cut.
  • Never the brightestOn dark paper the pigments lift and the ink lightens; a print never outshines the screen.
