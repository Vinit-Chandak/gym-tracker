What you asked, answered

Body first
Body leads the picker and Progress opens on it. The order is Body, Overview, Strength, Running, Recovery, History: you, then the record, then each subject, then the archive.
One total, not three
Overview's calendar and its totals are now one thing. Week, Month or Programme chooses the span; each week's sessions stand at the end of its row, and under the calendar every sport says what was done of what was planned, with its time and distance. Training totals, the month's strip and the weekly charts are gone, because this says all of it once.
Reps and RIR
Agreed: neither is charted any more. Heavier load and fewer reps is progress, so a reps line alone misreads it, and RIR is the effort the programme asks for, not a result. Each lift gets one strength line chosen by what it is, Volume beside it, and every set of every session keeps its load, reps and RIR.
Filters
The funnel and the dates in the header go; only History keeps a filter sheet. Each section has one range row, right above what it changes. It names the dates actually drawn and offers only the ranges your data fills. Seven days of weight get no buttons at all, just “17 – 23 Sept · all 7 readings”.
The next step
Body: Log today's weight. Overview: today and the days still to come are drawn as planned. Strength: every lift opens its sessions. Running: the next planned run leads the list. Recovery: Check in for today. History: open any entry.

  • Overview. One calendar for the chosen span, days ahead drawn as planned, each week's count at its row's end, then done of planned per sport.

    Gone: Training totals (now Programme), the month's totals strip (now the rows), Weekly sessions (now each row's count), the range caption.

  • Strength. Every lift in the range with its trend, latest figure and change, grouped by programme day with today's session first; a lift opens its line and every session's sets.

  • Running. Distance or Pace, one headline, the weeks, then every run, the planned and skipped ones included.

  • Recovery. Four averages that choose the chart, the chosen measure's readings, every reading behind View values.

    Gone: the second count, the Range average block (each tile is the average) and Recent check-ins (the same rows as View values).

  • History. As it is, which you were happy with. The only section that keeps the funnel, because it filters by activity, gym, exercise and machine.

Decided with you

  • The long span is the current programme, counted from its first day. With no programme running it is the last 12 weeks.
  • Totals compare done with planned. Days still to come are thinned icons on the calendar and a skipped part is dashed, so the next session is always in sight.

Rules the boards follow

  • A range is offered only when your data goes back further than it and it still holds two readings; All stands beside it. Here everything is under three months old, so 3M and 1Y would draw what All draws and are not offered. The longest range offered of 3 months or less is chosen first. The dates beside it say what is drawn; a tap on them sets exact dates.
  • One strength line per lift. Est. 1RM for barbell and dumbbell lifts prescribed at 10 reps or fewer (the app's own Epley rule). Heaviest set for machines, cables and higher-rep work, each machine its own line. Most reps for bodyweight; a weighted session is its own line. Longest hold for timed work.
  • Planned counts tasks, as the programme does: a day that lifts and runs plans one of each. Rides and swims outside the programme count as done and are never planned.
  • Every chart is ink and keeps its table of values. Pigment stays in the calendar's icons and the sports' marks.

Reading the canvas

  • The first row is each section as one whole scroll. The dashes in the margins mark where the phone's first screen ends.
  • Press Play on a board to click through: the section button opens the picker, a lift opens its page, Log today's weight opens its sheet, the period switch moves between Week, Month and Programme.
  • The programme and its prescriptions are the seeded 8-Week Strength + Aesthetics Hybrid. Runs, weights and check-ins come from the audit account. Which days were done is illustrative, worked out with the app's own rules: one programme day a day, Epley's estimate, and its muscle counting.
